Drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom. PNG rendering uses tiles to limit GPU memory, but the final image still uses system memory. For very deep detail, aim the camera at a specific opening and zoom progressively. This initial shader uses 32-bit floating point: iteration counts above ~12–15 may not produce new reliable geometric detail in a fixed global coordinate system.

The algorithm is an approximate distance-estimator ray marcher, not exact hierarchical traversal. Benchmark timings include GPU completion and pixel readback; they are not pure GPU kernel timings.
